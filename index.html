Income that feeds goals

Private credit positions are modelled at goal level — not just as portfolio yield. The fixed-income contribution to retirement probability is calculated and attributed directly.

Goal-level attribution shows which private credit positions are contributing to which goal probability. Not just yield — probability impact in basis points.
Maturity matching against goal target dates. Dubai Retail Partners matures Sep 2026 — feeds into the capital availability calculation for shorter-horizon goals.
Interest rate monitoring — when central bank rates shift, private credit floating-rate positions are flagged and their goal-level impact recalculated.
